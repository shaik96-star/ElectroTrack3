A clearer picture

Everything you need to
make sense of usage.

One place to keep records tidy and find the patterns that matter.

Manage consumer profiles

Store names, contact details, consumer types, regions, and notes. Link a consumer with the meters used at their property.

Keep meters organized

Manage multiple meters per consumer, with meter codes and details kept alongside each meter’s own reading history.

Record and validate readings

Enter dated cumulative kWh values or import a batch from CSV. Readings are checked in date order; decreases and unusually large jumps are flagged for review.

Explore usage patterns

View trends by period and filter analytics by consumer, region, type, meter, or data source. Compare consumers or meters over the selected period.

Catch records to review

Set a high-usage alert level and a separate reading-review threshold. Unusual readings can be approved or rejected before they affect usage totals.

Prepare useful reports

Review monthly totals, consumer and meter summaries, and reading-validation details. Export reports as CSV or print a report view.
